# Privacy Policy — Scholar Cite Copier

_Last updated: [FILL IN DATE BEFORE PUBLISHING]_

Scholar Cite Copier is a free browser extension that adds one-click citation
copying (APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, Vancouver, BibTeX) next to results on
Google Scholar. This policy explains what the extension does and does not do
with your data.

## Summary

Scholar Cite Copier does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal
data, browsing history, or search activity. It has no user accounts, no
analytics, and no advertising.

## What the extension does

- Adds buttons next to each Google Scholar search result's native "Cite"
  link.
- When you click one of these buttons, the extension triggers Google
  Scholar's own citation popup (the same one you'd see by clicking "Cite"
  yourself), reads the requested citation text out of it, and copies that
  text to your clipboard.
- For BibTeX specifically, the extension fetches the plain-text BibTeX file
  from the link Google Scholar itself provides in that popup.
- All of the above happens only when you click a button — the extension does
  not scan, log, or transmit your search queries or results in the
  background.

## What data is accessed, and why

| Data | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| The content of the Google Scholar page you're viewing | To find each result's native "Cite" control and read the citation text it provides |
| Clipboard write access | To copy the citation text you requested |

The extension's `host_permissions` are limited to Google Scholar's own
domains (`scholar.google.com` and its regional variants) plus
`scholar.googleusercontent.com`, which is where Google Scholar hosts BibTeX
files. No other websites are accessed.

## What the extension does NOT do

- It does not collect or transmit your searches, clicks, or browsing
  history to us or anyone else.
- It does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
- It does not require an account, sign-in, or license key.
- It does not make any network requests of its own beyond reading the
  Google Scholar page you're already on and fetching a BibTeX link that
  Google Scholar itself provides.
- It does not sell or share data with third parties, because no data is
  collected in the first place.

## Optional support link

The extension's popup includes an optional link to the developer's Ko-fi
page (https://ko-fi.com/larpstudios) for anyone who wants to support
development. Clicking it opens Ko-fi in a new tab; the extension does not
send Ko-fi any information about you, and using the extension's features
never requires visiting that link.

## Changes to this policy

If this extension's behavior changes in a way that affects what data it
accesses, this policy will be updated accordingly, and the "Last updated"
date above will reflect that.
